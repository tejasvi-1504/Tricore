# Assets

## Your photo

Drop your photo here and name it exactly:

    assets/mentor.jpg

**Recommended:** square crop, at least 800×800px, face centred, good lighting.
JPG or WebP. Under ~300KB after compression.

Until that file exists the site shows an initials avatar with a small
"Add your photo" hint — it never shows a broken image.

To use a different filename or a second person, edit the `<img src="...">`
inside the `.mentor-photo` block in index.html.
