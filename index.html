01 Sound familiar?

Four things that quietly waste a year

If two or more are true, an hour with someone who has been through it is worth more than another tutorial.

01

You keep starting, not finishing

Three courses at 40%. Two projects at “almost done”. Each one felt like the right call on the day you started it.

02

You measure yourself against the loudest person in class

Someone posted an internship. Now your plan feels wrong — even though you never had one written down.

03

You don’t know what companies actually check

So you prepare for everything, badly, instead of three things properly.

04

Nobody has seen your work and told you the truth

Friends say it looks good. Nobody who has sat on the other side of an interview table has looked at it.

02 After a month

What is actually different by the end

  1. One path, written down

    Not a list of everything. The specific next three months — and permission to ignore the rest.

  2. A profile that survives a real look

    Resume, GitHub and LinkedIn reviewed by someone who has screened them, not against a template.

  3. You have been interviewed properly, once

    Mock rounds where the feedback is specific and occasionally uncomfortable, which is the only kind that works.

  4. You know what you are not doing

    The hardest part, and the most valuable. Most students are stuck because they will not drop anything.

This is for you if

  • You are in 2nd or 3rd year — or 1st year and already exploring
  • You have tried things and they have not added up yet
  • You want an honest read, not encouragement
  • You will act on a plan once you have one

This is not for you if

  • You have not written a line of code yet — start free, come back
  • You want a job guarantee. Nobody honest sells one
  • You want someone to do the work for you
  • You are in final year expecting a placement in three weeks
