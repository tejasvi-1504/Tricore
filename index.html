What does the programme cost?

Two options. A 1-day trial is ₹200 for a single hour, so you can try it before committing. The full month is ₹2,000 right now (regular ₹3,000) and covers all four weekends — the Saturday learning sessions and the Sunday 1:1s.

I'm in first year and know nothing yet. Is that a problem?

That's exactly who this is for. First year is the best time to start — you have four years to use the plan we build together.

How do I pay?

Booking on this site opens WhatsApp with your details already filled in. Kanishka confirms your slot there and shares payment details directly — nothing is charged on the website. We're keeping it personal while we're small.

Every weekend for four weeks. Saturday is the group learning session and Sunday is your 1:1 time. Batches start on a Saturday, and you pick which one when you enrol.
