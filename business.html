How long does a website or CRM take?

A website 1–2 weeks, a store around 3, a CRM 4–8 depending on scope. You get a firm timeline in writing before we start.

What happens after the project is delivered?

Two to three months free, depending on the plan. After that, a small monthly arrangement or a clean handover — the code is yours either way.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes, on every project. Listed prices are exclusive of GST at 18%. EMI options are available on request.
